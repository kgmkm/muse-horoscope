# Compact keyword reference (built into the skill)

The full, source-checked library lives at https://github.com/kgmkm/astrology-library
(CC BY 4.0). This file is the minimum the skill needs offline. Sources: Alan Leo and
Sepharial (public domain) for the psychological side; Arlene Kramer's delineations,
summarised, for the Uranian points.

## Planets as functions
Sun: centre, purpose, vitality (Leo: the Life-Giver). Moon: feelings, safety, habit
(the Mother). Mercury: thought, words, curiosity (the Thinker). Venus: love, beauty,
harmony (the Unifier). Mars: action, desire, spark (the Energiser). Jupiter: growth,
optimism, learning (the Uplifter). Saturn: challenge, endurance, duty (the Subduer).
Uranus: change, originality, freedom (the Awakener). Neptune: dreams, imagination,
empathy (the Mystic). Pluto: depth, transformation, attachment (modern completion).

## Signs as styles (Leo keywords, condensed)
Aries: pioneer, courage, enthusiasm. Taurus: patience, endurance, duty. Gemini:
versatility, quickness, restlessness. Cancer: receptive, sensitive, nourishing.
Leo: magnanimity, command, heart-centred. Virgo: industry, objective mind.
Libra: balance, justice, harmony. Scorpio: intensity, regeneration, secrecy.
Sagittarius: restless, higher emotions, sport. Capricorn: ambition, prudence.
Aquarius: humane, intuition, firmness. Pisces: deep feelings, kind, psychic.

## Houses as arenas (Sepharial, condensed)
1 self and appearance. 2 money and possessions. 3 communication, siblings, short
trips. 4 home, roots, mother. 5 children, pleasure, creation. 6 health, daily work.
7 partners and contracts. 8 shared resources, inheritance, loss. 9 travel, belief,
publishing, law. 10 career, honour, father. 11 friends and groups. 12 hidden
matters, retreat, confinement.

## Aspects
Conjunction 0°: united, variable. Sextile 60°: opportunity, flows. Square 90°:
friction that demands work. Trine 120°: harmony, flows. Opposition 180°: tension,
awareness through polarity. Minor hard (45°, 135°): light friction, only when tight.

## Uranian points (German hypothetical bodies)
Cupido: gathering, family, groups, art. Hades: the past, buried things, depth.
Zeus: directed energy, creative drive. Kronos: authority, the recognised expert.
Apollon: expansion, increase, learning. Admetos: narrowing, deep specialisation.
Vulcanus: immense power. Poseidon: truth, spirit, illumination.
Read a pair as "B's themes working in A's territory". Midpoints: (A + B) / 2;
a body on A/B is written A/B = C and read as A and B working together at C.
